# 06 · Heroes, Epic Units, Superweapons & Support Powers

> Bible §33–36. Data: [`../data/combat-rules.json`](../data/combat-rules.json), [`../data/timings.json`](../data/timings.json).

## Heroes / commandos (§33)

Memorable but **not mandatory**. Rules: one at a time · high cost · long rebuild delay · strong specialized role · vulnerable to vehicles/focused fire · **cannot destroy a base alone** · abilities require skill and timing. A hero **enhances** an army, it doesn't replace one.

## Epic units (§34)

A battlefield event. Cost ≈ **7–10 standard tanks + substantial tech**. Strength: strong frontal durability, multiple weapons, an army-support ability, psychological presence.

**Each epic unit needs ≥3 weaknesses:** slow movement · no air defense · vulnerable rear · EMP susceptibility · limited turning · high repair cost · visible construction · specialized counters.

**Rule:** an equal-cost mixed army using correct counters should defeat an **unsupported** epic unit.

## Superweapons (§35)

Exist to prevent endless defensive games. **Required properties:** visible facility · discoverable countdown · global warning near launch · clear target indicator · evacuation opportunity · long rebuild & recharge · **countered by destroying the facility.**

**Damage standard:** may destroy light structures at center, severely damage production, damage heavy buildings, force army movement, disable an area. It must **not** erase a complete healthy HQ complex in one strike.

**Timing:** construction no earlier than Tier 3 · initial activation 4–7 min · recharge 5–8 min.

## Support powers (§36)

Create tactical opportunities, not a substitute for production. Standard power ≈ **1–3 units** of value; high-tier ≈ **4–6 units** — only when aimed and timed correctly. Large powers must telegraph: audio warning · ground marker · delay · visible incoming effect. **The target must have some reaction opportunity.**
