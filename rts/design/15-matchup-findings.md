## Root causes

### A · No armor-type interactions (data) — the big one
Every unit currently takes near-full damage from every damage type, so **raw DPS-per-cost decides fights**. That produces two backwards results:
- **Cheap infantry mass wins.** At 3000 cr, 15 Riflemen out-DPS 3 tanks (588 vs 170) and win — because a tank cannon isn't penalised against dispersed infantry.
- **Anti-armor units lose to tanks.** Missile Trooper / Lancer deal too little sustained DPS relative to a tank that shreds them at full effectiveness.

**Fix — adopt an armor-archetype resistance convention** so the §25/§9 weaknesses are actually in the numbers:

| Armor type | Resists (takes less) | Vulnerable (takes full/bonus) |
|---|---|---|
| **Infantry** | antiArmor, siege (AP/shells overkill a single soldier) | smallArms, explosive, energy |
| **Light vehicle** | smallArms | antiArmor, explosive |
| **Heavy vehicle / tank** | smallArms, explosive | **antiArmor**, siege |
| **Structure** | smallArms, antiArmor | siege, air |
| **Aircraft** | — | dedicated AA (air-defense) |

This makes tanks *bad* against infantry, anti-armor *strong* against tanks, and artillery *strong* against structures — turning the intended counters into real ones.

### B · Shield-break should bypass shields (mechanic)
The Disruptor and Nullifier are *shield-disruptors*, but the Aegis Tank's 30 % energy resist blunts their energy damage, so they lose to the exact unit they're built to kill. **Fix — add an `ignoreResist` (shield-break) flag** so those weapons ignore the target's energy/shield resistance.

### C · Sim model limits (not data bugs)
The simulator is a first-order **sustained-DPS attrition** model. It under-values things whose worth isn't sustained DPS:
- **Artillery** (Howitzer) loses a DPS race it should never be in — its value is **alpha strike + range** vs clustered/static targets, not trading blows.
- **Anti-air** over/under-reads because there's no **range / first-strike / retreat** (§27 "shouldn't annihilate before a retreat attempt").

**Fix — model refinements** (next tool iteration): burst/alpha, range & first-strike, and a retreat threshold. Until then, judge artillery/AA matchups by hand rather than trusting the raw band.

## Recommended next pass (per §47 "smallest solution, observe")
1. Encode the **armor-archetype table (A)** into each unit's `resist` — one systemic change, re-run the report.
2. Add the **shield-break flag (B)** to Disruptor/Nullifier and teach the sim to honor it.
3. Iterate the handful of still-off numbers until the counter matchups land in their §8 bands.
4. Only then extend the sim with **alpha/range/retreat (C)** and re-check artillery & AA.

Nothing here is a crisis — it's the framework working as designed: the rosters were a plausible first draft, and the tool turned "looks reasonable" into a concrete, prioritized tuning list.

## Pass 2 — resolved (A & B)

Applied the fixes and re-ran `matchup.mjs --report`: **0 of the sim-judgeable matchups now flag.**

- **A · Armor table** encoded in [`../data/armor-table.json`](../data/armor-table.json) and applied to all 45 units by [`../scripts/apply-armor.mjs`](../scripts/apply-armor.mjs) (class→archetype; each unit now carries an `armor` tag). Heavy armor takes bonus antiArmor damage; infantry resist AP; structures fold to siege, etc.
- **B · Shield-break** — `ignoreResist:true` on the Disruptor & Nullifier; the sim bypasses the target's resistance for them.
- **DPS tune** — the slow anti-armor/shield units were under-firing for the attrition model; nudged Missile Trooper (reload 3.0→2.0, dmg 60→68), Nullifier (dmg 240→300, reload 3.6→2.8), Disruptor (dmg 70→95, reload 3.0→1.7).

**Result:** Missile Trooper→MBT **30%**, Nullifier→Aegis **29%**, Disruptor→Aegis **31%** — all "strong counter" (25–45%). The MBT mirror stays neutral; Marauder still loses to the Vanguard; Lancer still loses 1v1 to the Bastion epic. Rosters remain validated 0/0 and the lab is rebuilt with the new numbers.
