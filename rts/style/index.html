RTS · Visual Style Guide

Slightly Cartoon, but Detailed

Cartoon silhouette, detailed surface. Bold, chunky, instantly-readable shapes at RTS zoom — with the detail carried in the surface (panel lines, rivets, glow) rather than a busy outline. Readable like a cartoon, finished like a model kit.

Style Pillars

01

Silhouette first

Identifiable by outline alone at full zoom-out. Distinct per role and faction.

02

Clean outlines

~2–3px dark outline (#0E1116) on major forms; thinner interior lines.

03

Cel + one detail pass

Body tone · shadow · highlight · soft occlusion, then panel lines & rivets.

04

Chunky proportions

Oversized cannons, heavy treads and feet — pushed past realism, still grounded.

05

20% team color

Faction color covers ~a fifth of the unit; the rest is a desaturated body.

06

Always grounded

A soft contact shadow under every unit for readability on any terrain.
