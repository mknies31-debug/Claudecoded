# Video Research Questionnaire

Answer these when you drop in a video. Say it out loud like **"1A, 2C, 3B, 4D, 5 the price claim."**
Skip any you don't care about and I'll use the default (in bold).

---

### 1. Why are we looking at this video?
- **A. Competitor check** (what they're selling and how)
- B. Content idea (I want to make my own version)
- C. Vehicle research (a car I might buy or sell)
- D. A customer sent it to me

### 2. What should I pull hardest?
- A. The hook, script and style (first 3 seconds, how they talk, how they close)
- B. Vehicles, prices and specs
- C. The comments (what viewers ask, like, and push back on)
- **D. Everything**

### 3. Who is it for?
- **A. Buyers**
- B. Private sellers (buy-side)
- C. Trade-in customers
- D. Just me

### 4. What do you want back?
- **A. Quick 5-line summary**
- B. Full breakdown
- C. A script for my own version
- D. A customer message (Direct, Softer, NEPQ)

### 5. Anything I should watch for?
Free answer. A vehicle, a claim to fact-check, a price to compare, or "nothing."

---

**Command version:**

    python extract.py "<video link>" --comments --frames 6 --brief "1A 2C 3B 4D 5 the price claim"

The answers print at the top of the report, so the brief stays with the research.
